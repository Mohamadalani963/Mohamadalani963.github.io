Make It Perfect! · Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

The short version
  • No account, no sign-in, no name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • We collect anonymous gameplay statistics to improve the game. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Players under 13, and anyone who has not told us their age, only get ads that are child-directed and not personalized.
  • We never sell data.

This policy explains what information the Android game Make It Perfect! (“the game”), published by Damas Game Studio (“we”, “us”), handles, why, and the choices you have. The game is designed for players of all ages, including children.

1. Information stored only on your device

The game saves your progress (levels, coins, hints, completed rooms) and your settings (sound, music, haptics, anonymous statistics) in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us.

Age screen. After the first room, the game asks once for your year of birth. It is used only on your device to choose an age group (under 13, 13–15, or 16 and over). Only the age group is saved; your year of birth is not stored and is never sent to us.

Device backup. If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the game’s save file in your device backup to your Google account. This is controlled by Android and Google, not by us, and you can turn it off in your device settings.

2. Anonymous gameplay statistics

To learn which levels are too hard or too easy, the game sends anonymous events to our analytics provider, PostHog (servers in the United States). This is on by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings → Anonymous stats.

What is sentDetails
Gameplay eventsGame and session start/end (with session length), level started, completed or restarted, hint used, room completed — with the level number, puzzle type, difficulty, room, time taken, number of moves and whether a hint was used.
Technical informationApp version, Android version, device model.
A random install IDA random number created by the game on your device. It is not linked to your name, account, advertising ID or any other app. It is deleted when you turn statistics off (a new one is made if you turn them back on).

We do not send your name, email, contacts, location, advertising ID, age or age group, or anything you type. We configure PostHog not to create personal profiles, not to look up location, and to discard IP addresses. Statistics are only used, in aggregate, to improve the game, and are kept for a limited period (no longer than 12 months). Events not yet sent are kept on your device until there is an internet connection, and are deleted immediately if you turn statistics off.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional “watch an ad” rewards (for example a hint or skipping a level) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Ads are never required to play. Google may collect and process information as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

How ads are requested depends on the age group from the age screen:

Age groupAds
Under 13, or age not answered yetRequests are marked as child-directed and under the age of consent. Ads are not personalized, the advertising ID is not used for them, and ad content is limited to a “G” (general audiences) rating. No consent form is shown.
13–15Requests are marked as under the age of consent; ads are not personalized.
16 and overAds may be personalized. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), Google’s consent form asks for your choice first. You can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy options. Personalized ads may use your device’s advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your Android settings.

4. Google Play services

The game asks Google Play whether a newer version is available so it can show an “Update available” button. This check is handled by the Google Play Store app under Google’s Privacy Policy; the game sends us nothing about it.

5. Children’s privacy

The game is suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: there is no account, no chat, no sharing and no way to enter personal details. For players under 13 (and anyone who has not answered the age question), ads are child-directed and not personalized, as described above. The anonymous statistics contain no personal information and can be turned off by a parent in Settings. We designed the game to follow the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR’s rules for children.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent any data. Information is only processed by the service providers named above (PostHog for anonymous statistics, Google for ads and Play services), for the purposes described here.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off statistics: Settings → Anonymous stats.
  • Change ad consent (16+, where applicable): Settings → Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your Android settings.
  • Delete everything on your device: uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android settings.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or U.S. state laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because the statistics we receive are anonymous and not linked to you, we usually cannot identify which data is yours; contact us and we will help as far as we can.

8. Security

Statistics are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your save file stays in the game’s private storage on your device.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game’s update notes.
